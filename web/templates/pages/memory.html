A cue (observation, message) is matched against memories by full-text rank{{if .Data.Semantic}}, embedding similarity{{end}}, recency, activation, importance and source credibility. The fused score competes for attention like any thought; admitted recalls are reinforced. Decay lowers activation of unused memories but never deletes them.
